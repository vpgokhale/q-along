🎙️ Q-Along

Your browser doesn't support live speech recognition (the Web Speech API), so it can't listen and check your answers — this is a Safari limitation (Safari has never implemented this API, on desktop or iOS). Reading prompts aloud (text-to-speech) still works here, but for the full experience — listening to you read questions and answer choices — use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on desktop, with an internet connection and microphone access.

Reading pace: WCPM
i
The device leads: it reads a short phrase (2–3 words) aloud using its built-in text-to-speech, then waits for the reader to repeat it back before reading the next phrase. Unlike Helper mode, this isn't triggered by getting stuck — the device sets the pace the whole time, at the words-per-minute rate above. Turning this on turns off Helper mode, since the device is already leading.
